# Stats reel topic bank (pick one not used in tools/stats-log.csv; rotate categories, never the same category two days in a row)
Categories and ideas (icons only, no faces):
- money-then-vs-now: 1990/2000 vs 2026 prices (cinema ticket, Maggi, train fare, rent, salary, iPhone vs phone, gold per decade)
- cricket: most ODI/T20/Test runs, wickets, sixes, centuries; IPL titles by team; World Cup wins by country (names in text, no photos)
- india-states-cities: population, area, literacy, GDP per state, most visited cities, tallest buildings, longest rivers/bridges
- world-vs-india: biggest economies, most populous countries, Olympic medals, internet users, fastest trains
- bollywood-box-office: highest-grossing Hindi films by year/all-time (titles only, no posters), most expensive films
- youtube-internet: most subscribed Indian channels, most viewed Indian videos, app downloads, smartphone users by year
- food-fun: chai consumption, biryani orders, most eaten snacks, sweets per festival
- space-science: ISRO missions cost vs Hollywood films, tallest statues, fastest animals, deepest/highest places
- money-world: richest countries per capita, salaries by profession in India, cost of weddings, petrol price by country
- festivals-season: tie to what is trending this week (Diwali sales, IPL auction, exams, monsoon rainfall by city)
